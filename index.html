Our Services

Comprehensive Emergency Dental Services

From severe pain relief to complex repairs, our 24/7 clinic is equipped to handle all dental emergencies in Glendale, AZ.

Severe Toothache Relief

A severe toothache is often a sign of an underlying issue like decay or infection. We provide urgent tooth care to diagnose the source of the pain and deliver immediate, effective relief so you can get back to your normal life.

Abscess & Infection Treatment

Dental infections and abscesses can cause dangerous swelling and intense pain. We provide prompt drainage, antibiotic therapy, and definitive treatment to eliminate the infection and protect your overall health.

Broken & Chipped Tooth Repair

Dental injuries from accidents or sports can chip or break a tooth. We offer emergency broken tooth repair, using bonding, crowns, or other methods to restore the function and appearance of your smile immediately.

Lost Fillings & Loose Crowns

A lost filling or loose crown can expose sensitive tooth structures to bacteria and temperature changes. We provide same-day replacement and recementing services to protect your tooth from further damage.

Emergency & Same-Day Root Canal

When the pulp of the tooth becomes infected, a root canal is often the only way to save it. We offer emergency root canal treatment and same-day root canals when appropriate, relieving pain and preserving your natural tooth.

Swelling & Gum Disease Care

Sudden swelling in the gums or face is a dental emergency. We provide urgent evaluation and treatment for acute gum disease, periodontitis, and other causes of oral swelling to prevent the spread of infection.
